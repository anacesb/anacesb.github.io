My current research develops fast, high-resolution quantitative cardiac MRI, with a focus on the atria and on contrast-free myocardial tissue characterization, combining MR Fingerprinting, image reconstruction, and deep learning.

Contrast-free myocardial scar assessment
------
<!-- ![Synthetic scar-weighted images from native MRF maps](/images/research/synthetic-lge.png) -->
<!--
Late gadolinium enhancement (LGE) is the clinical standard for detecting myocardial scar, but it requires a contrast agent. Using native (pre-contrast) cardiac MRF T1 and T2 maps, I generate synthetic images with contrast optimized to separate scar from viable myocardium, without gadolinium.

*Related work:* [ISMRM 2024 (digital poster)](https://submissions.mirasmart.com/ISMRM2024/Itinerary/ConferenceMatrixEventDetail.aspx?ses=D-60)
